## Record Format

Each line retains its origin, core idea, generated question, related branches, tests, status, and reason
for continuation or closure in the archived full entry. Statuses are historical records, not a new
research decision made during this cleanup.

## Separation Rule

A line of thought is not a formal result. Requirements, experiments, results, failures, and open gates
remain separate records and preserve their original branch and PGA identifiers.
