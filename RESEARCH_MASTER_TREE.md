# Research Master Tree

## 1. Research Objective

Explore whether relational and observational structures motivated by Indian knowledge traditions can
be expressed as testable mathematical models compatible with established physics. This is a navigation
and lineage map, not a scientific conclusion.

## 2. Core Research Tree

```text
Indian knowledge traditions and mathematics
  -> analogies (A001-A020)
  -> lines of thought (L001-L006)
  -> requirements (R001-R006)
  -> experiments (E001 and later, grouped by L###)
  -> results (positive, partial, negative, failed, or open)
```

The historical numbered branches `10_LOT_ACCOMMODATION_RELATIONAL_EMERGENCE` and
`11_EPR_BELL_CHANDAS` are preserved in `archive/` and retain their original identifiers.

## 3. Active Branches

The existing line-of-thought index records L001-L006 and their statuses. This restructuring does not
select or reprioritize a branch. See [LINES_OF_THOUGHT.md](LINES_OF_THOUGHT.md).

## 4. Completed / Established Branches

No new branch is classified here. Existing experiment and result records retain their own status and
limitations in [experiments/](experiments/) and [results/](results/).

## 5. Failed / Rejected Branches

Negative results, closed mechanisms, and abandoned paths remain attached to their original records;
they are not deleted or rewritten as successes.

## 6. Deferred Branches

Deferred and open questions remain deferred in their source records. No new checkpoint is selected.

## 7. Cross-Branch Relationships

L001, L004, and L006 explicitly share ordered-coverage and accessibility questions. L002 approaches
emergent geometry through capacity/counting, while L003 and L005 retain channel/coupling questions.
These relationships are historical links, not a merger of branches.

## 8. Research / PGA Index

PGA identifiers are provenance labels preserved in the archived branch documents and result records.
They are not promoted to independently validated experiments by numbering alone.

## 9. Current Research Checkpoint

The source master tree documents `PGA 1.12.6.1-J completed` with propagation of persistent
organization described as the next gate. The source record is preserved; this restructuring neither
endorses that checkpoint nor chooses its successor. Existing records contain other checkpoint language
and any conflict requires later human review.

## 10. Open Foundational Questions

- What interaction law, if any, is meant by S-infinity/B-infinity?
- Which properties are derived, and which remain assumptions?
- Can any proposed structure survive controls and reproduce independently?
- Which claims are mathematical toy-model results rather than physical derivations?
