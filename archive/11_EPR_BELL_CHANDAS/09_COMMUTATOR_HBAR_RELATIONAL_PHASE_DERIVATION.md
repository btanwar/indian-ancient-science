# PGA 1.12.2.10.19 — Quantum Commutator and ħ from Relational Phase/Coverage Dynamics

## Objective

Attempt to derive the quantum commutator and the scale ħ from the same relational phase mechanism, rather than inserting canonical quantum mechanics at the start.

## 1. Start from the derived phase

The Meru quadratic projection gives a unit-circle state

  X²+Y²=1,

represented by

  z=e^{iθ}.

The phase θ is therefore a relational coordinate generated by the quadratic projection.

## 2. Ordered coverage

Let an observable relational configuration evolve through ordered frames. Let λ denote the primitive relational coverage parameter.

A phase mode may be written

  ψ(λ)=R exp(iθ(λ)).

A translation in λ acts as

  T(δλ)ψ(λ)=ψ(λ+δλ).

For a differentiable phase mode,

  T(δλ)=1+δλ d/dλ+O(δλ²).

The generator of translations is therefore

  G_λ=-i d/dλ.

This is derived as the generator of relational coverage translations.

## 3. Canonical pair attempt

Suppose a second relational coordinate x labels the spatial/arrangement projection of the same state, while λ labels ordered coverage.

If translation in x is generated by

  G_x=-i d/dx,

then the canonical momentum candidate is

  p=κ G_x=-iκ d/dx,

where κ is a dimensional conversion constant.

The commutator follows algebraically:

  [x,p]=iκ.

This is the quantum commutator form.

## 4. Can κ be identified with ħ?

Dimensional analysis gives

  [x,p]=action.

Therefore κ has dimensions of action.

If the physical action unit is fixed by the relational phase-to-coverage conversion, then κ can be identified with ħ:

  κ=ħ.

However, this identification is NOT derived yet.

The derivation has reduced the problem from "why does the commutator exist?" to:

  derive the universal phase/coverage action scale.

## 5. Phase-space interpretation

For a state

  ψ=A exp(iθ),

define the relational action coordinate

  S=κθ.

Then

  p=∂S/∂x=κ ∂θ/∂x.

Thus the familiar phase-action relation

  ψ∼exp(iS/ħ)

appears as a consequence of converting the dimensionless relational phase θ into an action coordinate.

If κ=ħ, this becomes the standard quantum phase relation.

## 6. Poisson-to-commutator route

At the classical relational level, suppose the state-space geometry has canonical coordinates (x,p) with Poisson bracket

  {x,p}=1.

Quantization by the phase generator gives

  [x,p]=iκ.

Thus the quantum commutator can be understood as the operator representation of the underlying relational phase-space translation algebra.

But deriving the canonical symplectic form itself from S∞↔B∞ remains open.

## 7. What succeeds

🟢 The existence of a commutator can be generated from translation generators of the derived phase/coverage structure.

🟢 The commutator coefficient necessarily has dimensions of action.

🟢 ħ can therefore be interpreted as the universal conversion scale between dimensionless relational phase and physical action, if such a universal scale is derived.

🟡 The universal numerical value of ħ is not derived.

🟡 The canonical x-p structure is not yet derived from S∞↔B∞.

## 8. Connection to Schrödinger dynamics

If the generator of ordered coverage evolution is H,

  iκ ∂ψ/∂τ=Hψ.

With κ=ħ:

  iħ ∂ψ/∂t=Hψ.

Thus the same constant that appears in the commutator also appears in the Schrödinger equation.

This unifies two normally separate quantum ingredients:

  phase/coverage generator
       ↓
  action scale κ
       ↓
  commutator + Schrödinger dynamics.

## 9. Critical result

The research has not yet derived the numerical value of ħ.

But it has produced a sharper interpretation:

  ħ is not necessarily a mysterious independent quantum postulate.

It may be the conversion constant between:

  dimensionless relational phase
  and
  physical action accumulated along ordered relational coverage.

This is a testable theoretical target.
