# Line of Thought L005 — Complementary-Channel Unification (Audio/Video, GW/EM)

**Status:** OPEN — largely accounted for by known physics; residual-term question unresolved
**Domain:** physics (gravitational waves, electromagnetism, multi-channel observation)

## Goal

Determine whether treating gravitational and electromagnetic phenomena as two complementary
observational "channels" (in the sense of an audio track and a video track) reveals any coupling
term or observational relationship that is **not** already explained by established
Einstein–Maxwell / gravitational-wave–electromagnetic-conversion physics.

## Why this analogy was combined on its own

Unlike L001–L004, this line rests on a single analogy because its testable content (a residual
coupling/observational term between two channels) is self-contained and does not depend on the
ordered-coverage, capacity, or hidden-state mechanisms explored in the other lines, even though it
originated from the same broader chat-archive discussion.

## Current status detail

- Dual-channel and complementary-channel reconstruction tests were run historically (`PGA-152`
  "Dual-Channel Reconstruction Test", `PGA-153` "Complementary-Channel Reconstruction Test"),
  and a later, more disciplined gravitational-wave/electromagnetic coupling and
  polarization-invariant experiment sequence exists in
  `indian-philosophy-modern-physics/V2/experiments/V2.7-gravitational-wave-em-coupling` and
  `V2.8-gw-em-polarization-invariant`.
- Explicit conclusion already on record: known physics (established Einstein–Maxwell structure and
  known gravitational-wave/electromagnetic conversion effects, e.g. around compact objects and
  pulsars) already accounts for observed coupling; **no confirmed additional/residual term has been
  established** (\(indian-mythology-modern-science/research/FAILED_{AND}_ABANDONED_PATHS.md\), "Direct
  Damru/VCR EM-GW novelty").
- This residual-term test is recorded as requiring reproduction and an explicit known-physics audit
  before any novel claim (\(research/REPRODUCTION_{QUEUE}.md\), R013).

## What this line of thought must NOT claim

- That gravitational waves are literally sound, or that electromagnetic waves/light are literally
  video.
- That any coupling observed in the historical dual-channel tests is novel until it has been shown
  to exceed what Einstein–Maxwell theory and known GW–EM conversion processes already predict.
