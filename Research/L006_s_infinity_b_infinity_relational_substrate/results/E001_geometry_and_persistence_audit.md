# Result E001 - Geometry and Persistence Audit

**Runbook step:** [../runbook.md](../runbook.md) step #1
**Experiment:** `experiments/E001_geometry_and_persistence_audit/`
**Status:** NEGATIVE

## What was tested

Whether generic relational connectivity produces stable 3D geometry and whether localized excitations persist without an ad hoc stabilizing mechanism.

## Method

Historical S-infinity/B-infinity E001 review covering random/self-organizing relational networks, nonlinear lattice/wave models, coupled modes, and topological persistence controls.

## Actual output

The source reports that relational networks did not robustly produce stable 3D geometry without additional assumptions. Localized propagation was obtained in some models, but persistence was generally lost without stabilization. A massless/massive normal-mode split and the phi-four kink were identified as known physics controls, not evidence for S-infinity/B-infinity.

## Interpretation

The broad substrate claim is not supported by the historical tests. Known mechanisms remain useful controls and possible ingredients, but they do not validate the umbrella hypothesis.

## Pass / fail

R006 requirements #1 and #2 are FAILED for the tested historical constructions. The failures do not prove that every possible substrate model fails.

## Next action

Preserve these negative results and only test a new construction with explicit assumptions and controls.
