# Experiment E001 - Geometry and Persistence Audit

**Source:** `indian-philosophy-modern-physics/V3/research/S_INFINITY_B_INFINITY/experiments/E001_s_infinity_b_infinity_origin.md` and `MATHEMATICAL_MODELS.md`
**Line:** L006 / R006 requirements 1-3
**Status:** HISTORICAL AUDIT; NEGATIVE FOR TESTED CONSTRUCTIONS

## Questions

1. Can relational connectivity produce stable extended 3D geometry without supplied coordinates?
2. Can a localized excitation propagate and persist without an ad hoc stabilizer?
3. Can observer reconstruction avoid supplied spatial landmarks?

## Extracted model forms

Relational substrate:

`G=(V,E)` or `S={G,L,U,O,D,A}`.

Causal reconstruction target:

`C=(E_events, relation_order, rho_density) -> candidate g_mu_nu`.

Persistent manifestation candidate:

`Psi(t_1) != Psi(t_2)` while `M(t_1) approximately M(t_2)`.

## Historical findings

- Random/self-organizing relational networks did not robustly produce stable 3D geometry without additional assumptions.
- Localized propagation was obtained in some nonlinear models, but persistence was generally lost without stabilization.
- A massless/massive normal-mode split was recognized as known coupled-field behavior.
- A high observer reconstruction correlation near `rho=0.9966` was invalid as evidence because landmark structure was supplied.
- The phi-four kink was retained as a known topological persistence control, not evidence for S-infinity/B-infinity.

## Decision

The tested constructions do not satisfy the umbrella substrate claim. This is a preserved negative audit, not a proof that every future substrate model fails.
