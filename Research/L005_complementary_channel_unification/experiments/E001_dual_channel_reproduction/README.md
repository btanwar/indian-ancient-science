# Experiment E001 - Dual-Channel Reconstruction

**Source:** `indian-philosophy-modern-physics/V3/shared/test/PGA-152.md`, `PGA-153.md`, and related chat extraction records
**Line:** L005 / R005 requirement 1
**Status:** HISTORICAL PROTOCOL; REQUIRES_REPRODUCTION

## Question

Can the historical dual-channel and complementary-channel reconstructions be rerun with explicit parameters before any GW/EM novelty claim is made?

## Extracted protocol

Represent one underlying event/state `S` with two observation maps:

\(E = Pi_{EM}(S)\),
\(G = Pi_{GW}(S)\).

A joint reconstruction is:

`R = R(E,G,phi)`.

The channel labels are abstract. They do not mean gravitational waves are literal audio or electromagnetic waves are literal video.

## Required controls

- Same underlying event for both channels.
- Independent single-channel reconstructions.
- Joint reconstruction comparison.
- Packetization and representation controls.
- Established Einstein-Maxwell/GW-EM baseline.
- Residual analysis before any novelty claim.

## Current state

The source archive preserves the experiment identifiers and conceptual protocol, but not a complete executable, parameter bundle, or raw output. This folder therefore records the derivation and protocol only; it does not claim a local reproduction.
