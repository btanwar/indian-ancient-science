# Axioms

This is the canonical inventory of foundational statements. It does not promote a repeated modelling
assumption into an axiom and does not treat analogy as evidence. The detailed source record is
preserved in `archive/10_LOT_ACCOMMODATION_RELATIONAL_EMERGENCE/`.

## Axioms

### A-001 — Relational configuration

A physical relational system is represented at a given stage by a configuration `C` in a space of
admissible configurations. Coordinates, metric, particles, spacetime, and gravity are not assumed
primitive by this statement.

### A-002 — Allowed relational transition

An admissible configuration may undergo an allowed transition `T: C_i -> C_(i+1)` that remains in the
configuration space. The form of `T` is not fixed by this axiom.

### A-003 — Observable distinction

The configuration or transition admits an operational map `O` to distinguishable information. This
does not specify an observer model, Born rule, or quantum measurement theory.

## Definitions

- `S-infinity` and `B-infinity` are working labels for a research hypothesis, not established entities.
- An analogy is a conceptual correspondence, not a mechanism, derivation, or evidence.
- A result is an outcome of a specified test and retains its limitations and provenance.

## Working Assumptions

Locality, conservation, symmetry, reversibility, stability, persistence, minimal change, elasticity,
coupling, physical time, distance, metric, Hilbert space, h-bar, gravity, and Einstein's equation
remain candidate properties or constraints unless a source record says otherwise.

## Open Hypotheses

- `C + T + O` may be a minimum working structure for relational dynamics with observable distinction.
- Persistence may require an invariant or other stabilizing mechanism; it does not follow from arbitrary
  transitions automatically.
- Relational dynamics may produce effective geometry or propagation, but those are separate tests.

## Derived Consequences

The source record derives the process `C_i -> T -> C_(i+1) -> O -> distinguishable result` as a
working logical chain. It does not establish physical spacetime, quantum mechanics, or gravity.

## Rejected / Superseded Assumptions

Earlier convenient assumptions remain rejected or superseded only where their source experiments say
so. They are preserved with their original explanations in the archive; this file does not rewrite
those records.
