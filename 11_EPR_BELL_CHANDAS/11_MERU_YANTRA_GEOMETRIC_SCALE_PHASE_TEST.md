## Test A — Nested enclosure count

The radial sequence 1 → 8 → 10 → 10 → 14 is generated by the constrained intersection geometry rather than being a simple arithmetic progression.

Cumulative counts are 1 → 9 → 19 → 29 → 43.

No justified physical mapping from these integers to phase, energy, frequency, or action has yet been established.

Result: 🟢 real geometric structure / 🟡 physical interpretation open.

## Test B — Traditional 96-unit scale

The reported traditional construction datum contains a 96-unit total scale and the proportion set 4:9:11:48.

Numerically, 96 = 2 × 48, while 4 + 9 + 11 + 48 = 72. Therefore the four listed proportional numbers are not themselves an additive partition of the stated 96-unit total. They should not be treated as four additive physical lengths without reconstructing the original construction rules.

A tempting identification such as 96 divisions → 2π phase would be an imposed interpretation, not a derivation.

Result: 🟢 candidate dimensionless construction scale / 🔴 no justified ħ identification.

## Test C — Meru elevation

A documented geometric reconstruction gives a 45-degree Meru elevation, implying height/base-width = 1/2.

This supplies a clean dimensionless geometric ratio. It may be useful as part of a scale hierarchy, but by itself it cannot generate a quantity with dimensions of action.

Result: 🟢 geometric ratio / 🟡 relevance to action scale open.

## Test D — What the geometry actually gives us

The strongest observation is not a particular number. It is the combination:

discrete constrained intersections → nested enclosures → central convergence → 3-D elevation.

This is structurally compatible with:

discrete relational configurations → constrained relational geometry → nested effective states → central/phase representation.

It does not yet establish that Meru geometry physically generates quantum mechanics or ħ.

## Action-scale test

The candidate relations remain:

κ ?= E_cycle / ω_cycle

κ ?= S_cycle / 2π

κ ?= p λ / 2π.

Meru geometry may supply the dimensionless cycle/phase structure, but the present evidence does not supply the absolute energy, frequency, momentum, or length scale.

Therefore κ has not yet been numerically derived.
