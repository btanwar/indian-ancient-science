# PGA 1.12.2.10.17 — General-Dimension and Composite-System Test

## Objective

Test whether the Meru mechanism that works for the two-component sector extends to arbitrary-dimensional quantum state spaces and composite systems without simply assuming Hilbert-space/tensor-product machinery.

## 1. Multinomial Meru structure

For N binary relational channels, Prastāra enumerates 2^N configurations.

Grouping by occupation counts produces multinomial coefficients. For amplitudes a_1,...,a_d,

  (a_1+...+a_d)^N

contains terms with multinomial multiplicities.

This establishes a natural combinatorial source for coarse-grained multiplicity and cross terms.

## 2. Candidate d-dimensional amplitude space

A candidate state is

  ψ=(z_1,...,z_d),

with z_i complex amplitudes and

  Σ_i |z_i|²=1.

Each complex coordinate can be viewed as the two-dimensional Meru quadratic pair

  z_i=X_i+iY_i.

The global normalization is then

  Σ_i (X_i²+Y_i²)=1.

Geometrically this is a real 2d-dimensional unit sphere.

The corresponding complex projective state space is obtained after identifying physically irrelevant global phase.

### Important result

The repeated two-dimensional quadratic construction naturally produces the dimension of a complex-amplitude state space:

  d complex amplitudes = 2d real quadratic coordinates.

But this is still a construction, not a uniqueness theorem.

## 3. Why projective identification is needed

Multiplication of every amplitude by a common phase,

  z_i → e^{iφ} z_i,

leaves every |z_i|² unchanged.

Therefore global phase cannot affect the quadratic measurement weights.

The observable state is consequently represented by an equivalence class

  ψ ~ e^{iφ}ψ.

This gives a candidate complex projective state space.

The global-phase redundancy is therefore structurally tied to the quadratic measurement rule.

## 4. General measurement

For a candidate normalized state ψ and a measurement direction φ,

  A(φ,ψ)=Σ_i φ_i*ψ_i

is the natural complex overlap.

Its quadratic magnitude is

  P(φ|ψ)=|A(φ,ψ)|².

For an orthonormal set {φ_k},

  Σ_k |<φ_k|ψ>|²=1.

Thus the Born rule follows from norm-preserving resolution into mutually exclusive orthogonal relational directions.

### Remaining issue

Orthogonality and completeness of arbitrary measurement directions are not yet derived uniquely from Meru. They remain the mathematical structure that must be generated by the relational substrate.

## 5. Composite systems

For subsystems A and B with d_A and d_B outcome classes, the joint relational configuration is a pair

  (i,j).

There are d_A d_B joint classes.

A joint amplitude array c_ij therefore has exactly the dimension expected from a tensor product:

  d_AB=d_A d_B.

Product states satisfy

  c_ij=a_i b_j.

Non-factorizable arrays are entangled.

This gives a combinatorial motivation for tensor-product dimension from pairwise relational configuration counting.

## 6. Entanglement

A shared relational state need not factorize even when the local measurement operations are independent.

This is precisely the project principle:

  operational isolation ≠ relational independence.

The Bell result already established that the Meru relational route can reach the quantum correlation benchmark.

## 7. No-signalling

For a joint probability

  P(A,B|a,b),

local marginal independence requires

  Σ_B P(A,B|a,b)=P(A|a)

independent of b, and similarly for B.

The existing Bell construction satisfies this for the standard cosine correlation.

General arbitrary-dimensional no-signalling must still be derived from the relational composition rule.

## 8. Attempted uniqueness test

Can Meru alone force complex quantum mechanics?

Not yet.

The same multinomial counting can support several mathematical state constructions:
- real amplitudes;
- complex amplitudes;
- potentially higher-dimensional algebraic representations.

The two-dimensional quadratic projection strongly motivates the complex plane because rotations are naturally encoded by X+iY. But that does not mathematically exclude other structures for d>2.

Therefore the correct conclusion is:

  Meru → complex QM is strongly motivated,
  but Meru → uniquely complex QM is NOT yet proven.

## 9. Critical new gate

The decisive test is now:

Can the following all be derived from one relational composition law?

1. orthogonality;
2. completeness;
3. complex phase;
4. quadratic probability;
5. tensor-product composition;
6. local transformation group;
7. noncommuting observables;
8. entanglement;
9. no-signalling.

If yes, the project would have a genuine first-principles route to quantum theory.

If not, the unexplained imported structure must be explicitly recorded.

## Verdict

**🟡 GENERALIZATION SURVIVES AS A CONSISTENT CONSTRUCTION, BUT UNIQUENESS IS NOT ESTABLISHED.**

The important advance is that the Meru mechanism scales naturally from the two-level circle to a candidate d-complex-amplitude state space and gives a combinatorial motivation for tensor-product dimension.

The next research frontier is therefore not another Bell calculation. It is deriving the relational composition law that forces the full quantum geometry and algebra.
