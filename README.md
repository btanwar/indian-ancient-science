# Indian Ancient Science

## Purpose

This repository records exploratory research connecting Indian knowledge traditions, mathematics,
philosophy, and modern science. It separates analogy, hypothesis, experiment, result, and failure.

## Research map

- [RESEARCH_MASTER_TREE.md](RESEARCH_MASTER_TREE.md) is the authoritative research lineage.
- [AXIOMS.md](AXIOMS.md) contains foundational statements and their status.
- [ANALOGIES.md](ANALOGIES.md) inventories analogies; analogy is not mechanism, derivation, or evidence.
- [LINES_OF_THOUGHT.md](LINES_OF_THOUGHT.md) preserves conceptual evolution.
- [experiments/](experiments/) contains tests and their source records.
- [results/](results/) contains outcomes, including negative results.
- [archive/](archive/) preserves superseded organization and historical source documents.

Templates remain in [templates/](templates/). The original prompt records remain in [promtps/](promtps/).

## Research discipline

Do not invent conclusions, promote assumptions into axioms, or treat analogies as evidence. Preserve
failed and uncertain work, retain historical identifiers, and trace every result to its experiment.

## Adding research

Add a focused record under `experiments/`, place its outcome under `results/`, and update the master
tree only when the existing record supports the change. Do not overwrite failed experiments.
