# Analogies

## Hilbert hotel — accommodation
An infinite hotel can re-index occupied rooms to accommodate another countably infinite population. Contribution: mathematical accommodation by global rearrangement. Physical caution: the mathematical mapping itself has no duration; physical duration would have to be introduced by a dynamical model.

## Beads on a string — connectivity and propagation
A local bead displacement can affect neighboring beads through the connecting string. Contribution: local disturbance can propagate through relations.

## Mala — persistent relational connectivity and topology
Many beads share one thread; a mala also motivates closed-loop topology. Contribution: separates local elements from their common relational structure. This is analogy, not evidence for physical spacetime topology.

## Sugar in water — redistribution
Localized sugar redistributes through water toward a new macroscopic configuration. Contribution: local-to-global redistribution, gradients, accommodation and equilibrium. It does not derive time.

## Damru / fabric — localized disturbance
A localized oscillatory element disturbs an extended medium. Contribution: local-to-global response. A physical fabric is not identified with spacetime.

## VCR / cinema / frame coverage
Ordered coverage can reconstruct a state without equating the reconstruction index with physical time. Contribution: keeps ordering distinct from physical time.

## Common abstraction
`local elements + relations + accommodation + propagation + ordered change`

Provisional B∞ abstraction: an extended relational structure capable of accommodating and propagating changes in local states.

## Provenance
These analogies are preserved as conceptual inputs to the S∞ ↔ B∞ exploration. None is treated as an independent physical derivation.
